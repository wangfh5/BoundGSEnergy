Challenge #49: Certified Heisenberg energy bounds

https://github.com/QuantumBFS/quantum.harness/issues/49

A unified structured-NPA and exact-compatible RG relaxation certifies the periodic N = 20 Heisenberg energy density within 5.2144e-6 and strictly improves both matched parent relaxations.

ChallengeApproachResultsHighlight

Challenge

The challenge asks whether structured NPA and renormalization-group compression can be fused into a scalable certification method for spin-1/2 Heisenberg systems. Structured NPA gives strong local moment constraints but becomes harder to solve as the system grows; RG compression carries consistency to larger physical regions using smaller PSD variables but is weaker on its own. The four official targets cover 1D and 2D Heisenberg and J1-J2 systems; the first is the periodic Heisenberg chain at N = 200 with a rigorous gap no larger than 1e-5.

Significance

A variational energy is only an upper bound. A complementary certified lower bound turns a numerical estimate into a rigorous interval and exposes whether the chosen local consistency constraints remain effective as the system grows.

The fusion is one SDP rather than a post-processing combination of two bounds. Structured-NPA Pauli moments reconstruct an eight-spin physical RDM; an independent PSD copy is linked coefficient-by-coefficient to that RDM; the audited dyadic RG map then generates compressed variables omega_4, omega_5, ... subject to recursive left- and right-marginal constraints. The matched RG-only and structured-NPA-only routes are controls, while the fused route contains both constraint families and optimizes the same Heisenberg energy. Replay rebuilds the complete formulation, binds the objective direction and every equality scale, and subtracts rigorous SOHS and RG-slack corrections.

Highlight

What's innovative

The fusion is a single exactly auditable relaxation, not a comparison or heuristic average of two bounds. A complete local Pauli-moment RDM is linked to an independent PSD witness before exact dyadic RG maps are applied, closing the physical-feasibility gap that ordinary floating-point coefficient assembly would leave.

Significance of the output

At the validation size, RG adds a small but strict improvement to an already target-level structured-NPA certificate. This establishes that the two constraint families are compatible and complementary when numerical conditioning and exact replay are handled together.

Next decision

The normalized N = 30, n_super = 8 model now solves, but ordinary moment residuals still erase its positive raw fusion gain during strict replay. The next task is to improve the numerical conditioning of the structured coefficient-matching equations without changing the relaxation. Larger runs are justified only after N = 30 passes the strict-improvement gate.
